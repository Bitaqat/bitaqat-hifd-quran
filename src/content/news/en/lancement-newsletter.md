Until now there was no way to be told when a new card, a tracking tool or a methodology update went
out. You had to come back to the site and compare. That is fixed: a newsletter is now available.

## What you will receive

One email whenever something genuinely new lands — new cards, a methodology revision, a new
tracking tool. Nothing else. No fixed schedule, and no message sent just to fill the silence.

## What we do with your address

It is used for those emails only. It is never shared, sold or passed on to anyone. Signing up
requires an email confirmation, so nobody can subscribe you against your will, and every message
carries an unsubscribe link that works in a single click.

The form sits at the bottom of every page, and on its own dedicated page.

## If you would rather avoid email

An RSS feed carries exactly the same news. It asks for no address and leaves no trace: just add the
feed URL to your usual reader.
